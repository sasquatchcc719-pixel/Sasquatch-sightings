## 3. Confidentiality, Customer Privacy, and Property Access Agreement

The employee may have access to customer names, phone numbers, addresses, access codes, lockbox information, gate codes, alarm information, job notes, pricing, invoices, photos, internal systems, company processes, and other private information.

The employee agrees to:

- Use customer and company information only for company work.
- Keep customer addresses, access instructions, codes, job notes, photos, invoices, and pricing private.
- Not copy, share, post, screenshot, or disclose customer or company information except as needed to perform assigned work.
- Not use customer information for personal reasons or outside work.
- Protect phones, tablets, passwords, apps, and any printed materials used for company work.
- Immediately report any lost device, exposed password, accidental disclosure, or security concern.
- Return or delete company and customer information when requested or when employment ends.

When entering a customer home, business, facility, or work area, the employee agrees to:

- Enter only areas needed to perform the assigned job.
- Respect customer privacy and property.
- Not open drawers, cabinets, rooms, closets, containers, or personal items unless clearly necessary for the job and authorized.
- Secure doors, gates, keys, lockboxes, and access codes.
- Report damage, unsafe conditions, pets, access problems, or unusual situations immediately.

These obligations continue after employment ends.

## 4. Timekeeping, GPS, Driving, Safety, and Equipment Acknowledgment

The employee agrees to accurately clock in and out and record all time worked. Working off the clock is not allowed. If a time entry is missed or incorrect, the employee must notify the company as soon as possible.

The employee understands that company systems may collect GPS/location information while the employee is clocked in, using company job tools, using company devices, traveling for assigned jobs, or otherwise performing company work. Location tracking is used for scheduling, dispatch, safety, job verification, payroll support, and customer service.

The employee agrees to:

- Use timekeeping and job systems honestly.
- Not clock in or out for another person.
- Not falsify location, job status, photos, notes, or completion details.
- Keep a valid driver's license if driving is part of the job.
- Drive safely and follow traffic laws.
- Not text, browse, or use handheld phone features while driving.
- Report accidents, tickets, vehicle damage, injuries, spills, chemical exposure, or unsafe conditions promptly.
- Use reasonable care with company equipment, chemicals, supplies, uniforms, keys, cards, devices, and documents.
- Follow label instructions, safety data sheets, and company instructions for cleaning chemicals.
- Use required protective equipment when appropriate.
- Return company equipment, uniforms, keys, cards, devices, and documents when requested or when employment ends.

The employee may be responsible for loss or damage caused by intentional misconduct, theft, dishonesty, or gross negligence, subject to applicable law.

## 5. Payment Handling Policy Acknowledgment

The employee may be asked to help collect or confirm customer payments for standard jobs. The employee agrees to follow company payment procedures exactly.

The employee agrees to:

- Use only approved company payment methods.
- Never write down, photograph, text, email, or store a customer's card number.
- Never enter raw card information into an unapproved app or personal device.
- Never accept off-book payments or personal payments for company work.
- Provide receipts or payment confirmation as instructed.
- Record payment status honestly.
- Report payment issues, failed payments, refunds, disputes, or customer concerns promptly.
- Keep customer pricing, invoices, and payment information private.

Some accounts, including facility or batch-billed jobs, may not allow field payment collection. If the company marks a job as no-payment-collection or hidden-price, the employee must not discuss totals, collect payment, edit price-bearing line items, or show invoice totals to the customer.
